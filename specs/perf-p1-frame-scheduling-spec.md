# Spec: Perf Phase 1 — Damage-driven frame scheduling (incl. amendment A1)

**Status:** planned on `feat/iterm2-parity` · **Author:** BSA (agent) · **Date:** 2026-08-27
**Track:** P (performance) · **Ships as:** 0.4.2 · **Size:** 1–2 days
**Depends on:** P0 (baselines) · **Independent of:** P3
**Type:** ghostty-web patch work — own commits, upstreamable to `coder/ghostty-web`
**Provenance:** derived from a local iTerm2 source read, 2026-08-27.

## Objective

Stop paying 60 fps for a terminal that has nothing to draw. This is iTerm2's third pillar
(damage-driven drawing: an idle window costs ~0% CPU) and, with P2.1, one of the two
highest-leverage single changes in the whole program.

## The gap, measured

ghostty-web runs a **free-running rAF chain** from `open()` to `dispose()` — ~60 fps forever,
even with zero output. The chain re-queues itself unconditionally from inside its own
callback (`node_modules/ghostty-web/dist/ghostty-web.js:2953–2955`), and `write()` schedules
nothing. As `src/lib/terminal/liveness.ts` puts it: *"the chain IS the display"*.

Measured: **~5% of a core per visible terminal at idle** — `IDLE_8S` script 0.420–0.477 s per
8 s, ~23× the previous engine (ADR-002:275). `docs/RELEASE-0.4.0.md` recorded this as
having "no fix short of upstream". It is fixable in the existing patch. iTerm2 idle: ~0%.

A *parked* terminal is already free (`pause()`/`resume()` patch, `dist:2804`/`:2828`, driven
by the LRU in `src/lib/terminal/cache.ts`). A **visible** terminal cannot be paused — that is
the floor this phase removes.

## User story

- As a developer with phasr open and idle, I want a visible terminal to cost effectively
  nothing, so the app is not the reason my laptop is warm and my battery is flat.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **`scheduleFrame()` replaces the self-requeuing chain.** A frame is requested by:
   `write()`, the cursor-blink tick, a scroll/selection/hover change, `resize`, and the DEC
   2026 synchronized-output timeout. A frame that finds nothing dirty performs no draw.
2. **The chain degrades to a ~1 Hz heartbeat; it never dies** (see A1 below). At idle,
   `getRenderStats().ticks` advances at ~1/s, not ~60/s.
3. **Adaptive cadence, driven by a throughput estimator**: 5 s history in 1/30 s buckets,
   exponentially weighted. Cadence:
   - **60 fps** while output **< 10 000 B/s**
   - **30 fps** above 10 000 B/s
   - **1 Hz** when idle for ~3 s, or when the surface is hidden (**"hidden" = the window is
     occluded / the app is backgrounded — see the Q6 decision below; "parked" is `pause()`'s
     job and is a different, stronger state**)
4. **Cadence *decreases* are deferred by one frame**, so the first screenful of a burst
   paints at full rate before the rate drops. (iTerm2 does this deliberately;
   `iTermUpdateCadenceController.m:294–302`.)
5. **Display updates are suppressed, not skipped, while a modal is up** — when the modal
   closes, the pending frame runs; the terminal does not wait for the next event.
6. **Cursor-blink timer runs only while the surface is focused.** An unfocused terminal
   requests no blink frames.
7. **Idle target met**: ≤ 0.5% of a core per visible terminal, and ~0 for hidden, measured
   per P0's apparatus (Activity Monitor on a packaged build for the headline number, WebKit
   probe for the regression signal).
8. **Cadence is observable**: under flood, `getRenderStats()` shows ~30 fps; typing at idle
   paints within 1 frame. Both assertable from a probe spec.
9. **No blind terminals.** `e2e/terminal-liveness.spec.ts` still passes; the watchdog
   (`STALL_DEADLINE_MS = 200`, `src/lib/terminal/liveness.ts:46`) never fires during normal
   idle operation, and a terminal left alone for 5 minutes still paints on the next
   keystroke.
10. **Patch hygiene**: dist changes land in their own commit(s), `patches/ghostty-web@0.4.0.patch`
    is regenerated by pnpm rather than hand-edited, and the hunks contain no phasr-specific
    types.

## #PATH_DECISION — heartbeat, not a dead chain (amendment A1)

The obvious implementation is: let the chain stop when nothing is dirty, and restart it from
the next event. The risk is a **dead chain → blind terminal** — a terminal that keeps focus,
keeps accepting keystrokes, keeps feeding the PTY, and never draws again. That failure has
already happened once here: *"one swallowed frame callback and the terminal is dead for the
rest of its life"* (`src/lib/terminal/liveness.ts`, measured in
`e2e/terminal-liveness.spec.ts`), which is why the watchdog exists at all.

**iTerm2 does not do that.** Its timers never stop; the cadence drops to a **1 Hz floor**
when idle or hidden, and a frame that finds nothing dirty simply doesn't draw.

**Decision: adopt iTerm2's shape — degrade to a ~1 Hz heartbeat rather than letting the
chain die.** Consequences:
- The residual idle cost is one no-op frame per second instead of sixty, which comfortably
  clears the ≤ 0.5%/core target.
- **The heartbeat *is* the liveness watchdog**, which removes this phase's main risk. Note
  in review whether `liveness.ts`'s watchdog can then be simplified — but do **not** delete
  it in this phase; it also covers webview suspension by sleep/occlusion/App Nap, which a
  heartbeat cannot.

Alternative considered and rejected: keep 60 fps and make the no-op frame cheaper. It does
not reach ~0 — the rAF delivery itself, plus `getCursor()` and the dirty scan, is the floor,
and it keeps the webview compositor awake.

## #PATH_DECISION — Q6: "hidden" means window-occluded / app-backgrounded, not parked

**Decision (2026-08-27, System Architect): in the cadence estimator, `hidden` = the *window*
is occluded or the app is in the background. "Parked" is a strictly stronger state that
`pause()` already owns, and the two must not be conflated.**

Three states, named so nobody re-derives them:

| State | Signal | Frames |
|---|---|---|
| **Parked** (off-screen tab, LRU-cached) | `setActive(false)` → the patched `pause()` (`dist:2804`), driven by `src/lib/terminal/cache.ts:42` and the three call sites (`Terminal.tsx:359`, `SessionTerminalTab.tsx:340`, `RunCommandTerminal.tsx:183`) | none at all |
| **Hidden** (visible surface, occluded/backgrounded window) | `document.visibilityState` + Tauri's window focus event | ≤ 1 Hz |
| **Visible** | neither | 60 / 30 / 1 Hz per the estimator |

Why the occlusion reading and not "parked": parked is already free, so the rule would be dead
code; occlusion is the uncovered case, and it is *phasr's* case — an agent flooding output into
a terminal the user cannot see, on a laptop lid-half-closed or behind another app.

**Both signals already exist in this codebase and must be reused, not reinvented:**
`document.visibilityState` is used by `src/lib/terminal/liveness.ts:181`,
`backends/ghostty.ts:1298` and `useCompletionNotifications.ts:115`, and the Tauri window
`onFocusChanged` listener at `useCompletionNotifications.ts:122`.

**Two consequences the implementer must handle, and they are the real content of this decision:**

1. **A hidden page's rAF does not fire.** WebKit and Chromium both freeze `requestAnimationFrame`
   for a hidden document, so a heartbeat built on rAF is 0 Hz while hidden, not 1 Hz. That is
   *fine for cost* and *fatal for A1's "the chain never dies" property* unless resumption is
   event-driven: the `visibilitychange` listener must kick the chain on the way back, which is
   exactly what `liveness.ts:181–182` already does (`if (document.visibilityState === "visible")
   checkAll("visible")`). Do not add a `setInterval` heartbeat to keep frames running while
   hidden — that would spend battery to preserve a mechanism whose only job is to notice
   staleness, and staleness while hidden is not observable.
2. **The watchdog must not read "hidden" as "stalled."** `healIfStalled()` already returns
   early when `document.visibilityState !== "visible"` (`backends/ghostty.ts:1297–1299`); the
   new cadence must keep that invariant true, and `e2e/terminal-liveness.spec.ts` is the guard.

Alternative considered and rejected: "hidden" = parked — redundant with `pause()`, and it
would leave the actual battery case (a backgrounded window full of streaming agents) at 60 fps.

## Test / evidence plan

- **vitest**: `src/lib/terminal/liveness.test.ts` and `options.test.ts` must stay green. Add
  unit coverage for the throughput estimator (bucket rotation, exponential weighting, the
  deferred-decrease rule) if it is implemented host-side; if it lives in the patch, cover it
  in the probe spec instead.
- **Playwright (default + WebKit)**: extend `e2e/terminal-liveness.spec.ts` with an idle-tick
  assertion (`getRenderStats().ticks` grows ~1/s at idle, ~60/s during a burst, ~30/s under
  flood) and a "5 minutes idle then a keystroke paints" case. Run under
  `pnpm test:e2e:webkit` as well — the paint cost is engine-specific.
- **Probe**: re-run `PHASE0_PROBE=1` (`e2e/terminal-phase0.spec.ts`) `IDLE_8S` and record
  before/after in the P0 baseline table.
- **Manual (the headline number)**: Activity Monitor on a packaged build, 1 visible terminal
  and 8-open/1-visible, per `docs/MANUAL-VERIFICATION.md`. **The e2e harness cannot produce
  the CPU-percentage number** — script time is a proxy, not the metric in the targets table.

`caffeinate`. Engine: ghostty-web 0.4.0 + `patches/ghostty-web@0.4.0.patch`
regenerated at this phase's commit.
